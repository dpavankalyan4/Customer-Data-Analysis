## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing customer shopping transactions across 10 shopping malls in Istanbul, Turkey, between 2021 and 2023.

The objective is to understand customer purchasing behaviour, identify shopping patterns, evaluate product category preferences, analyse revenue generation, and examine payment method usage.

Using SQL and Microsoft Power BI, the project transforms raw transactional data into meaningful business insights to support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyse customer shopping distribution by gender and age.
- Identify which customer segments purchase more products.
- Evaluate revenue contribution across different customer segments.
- Understand product category preferences.
- Examine relationships between payment methods and other business dimensions.
- Identify purchasing patterns across shopping malls.
- Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Derive actionable business insights and recommendations.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

The SQL analysis identified the following findings:

| Business Metric | Key Finding |
|---|---|
| Total Transactions | 99,457 |
| Total Products Sold | 298,712 |
| Total Revenue | 251.51 Million TL |
| Female Transactions | 59.81% |
| Male Transactions | 40.19% |
| Highest Product Quantity by Gender | Female |
| Highest Revenue by Gender | Female |
| Highest Product Quantity by Age Group | 20–29 |
| Highest Revenue by Age Group | 40–49 |
| Most Preferred Payment Method | Cash (44.69%) |
| Highest Performing Product Category | Clothing |

### Key Observations

- Female customers contributed a higher share of transactions, product purchases and revenue.
- Customers aged 30–39 recorded the highest shopping transaction count.
- Customers aged 20–29 purchased the highest quantity of products.
- The 40–49 age group generated the highest revenue among the analysed age groups.
- Clothing recorded the highest product quantity among the purchase categories.
- Cash was the most frequently used payment method.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, the following business recommendations can be considered:

1. **Customer Segmentation:** Develop targeted marketing campaigns for different age and gender segments.

2. **Product Strategy:** Focus on high-performing categories such as Clothing while evaluating opportunities in other categories.

3. **Revenue Optimization:** Explore premium product promotions targeting the 40–49 age group.

4. **Customer Engagement:** Design personalized offers to improve customer engagement and repeat purchases.

5. **Payment Strategy:** Maintain convenient cash payment options while encouraging digital payment adoption.

6. **Mall Performance:** Evaluate shopping patterns across individual mall locations to identify opportunities for improving sales performance.
